The Haunted Forest

A private themed dinner staged deep in an enchanted woodland of shadow and whisper.

FormatPrivate Themed Dinner
SettingEnchanted Woodland
GuestsSelect Gathering

The Evening

The Haunted Forest was staged as an exclusive dinner for a select gathering of guests, built around a single idea: what if the dining room itself became a woodland at night? Bare branches, hanging lanterns and drifting mist transformed the space into something between a fairy tale and a warning — beautiful, but not quite safe.

Smaller and more intimate than a full House of Kaos ball, this was theatre at close quarters — a dinner where the story unfolded course by course, and every guest was seated inside it rather than simply watching from the edge.

An exclusive dinner deep in an enchanted woodland of shadow and whisper.

The Setting

Every detail was placed with intent — from the table dressing to the half-glimpsed movement at the treeline. The Haunted Forest leaned on suggestion as much as spectacle, letting shadow and whisper do as much work as any costume.

Why It Mattered

Private dinners like the Haunted Forest sit at the more intimate end of what House of Kaos does — proof that the House's brand of immersive theatre works as well around a dinner table for a handful of guests as it does at a hundred-and-fifty-strong ball. It's a format we return to whenever the story calls for something smaller and closer.
