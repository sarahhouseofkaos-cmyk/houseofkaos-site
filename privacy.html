Legal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data House of Kaos collects through this website, why, and what your rights are. It isn't a substitute for legal advice, and we may update it from time to time — the version live on this page is the one that applies.

1. Who we are

House of Kaos Ltd (company number 16556191), registered in England and Wales, is the "data controller" responsible for your personal data under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

You can contact us about any privacy matter at hello@houseofkaos.co.uk.

2. What data we collect

We collect personal data in two main ways:

SourceData collected
Enquiry form on this websiteName, email address, the type of enquiry you select, and whatever you write in your message
Tickets booked through EventbriteHandled by Eventbrite as a separate data controller under its own privacy policy. House of Kaos doesn't receive or store your card details, and typically only sees the attendee information Eventbrite shares with event organisers (e.g. name and email)
Tickets booked directly with House of KaosName, email, and any booking or payment details needed to process your booking directly (e.g. for bank transfer bookings, your name and payment reference — we don't store full card details for direct bookings)

This website doesn't use analytics or advertising cookies, and doesn't track you across other sites.

3. How we use your data

We use the information you give us to:

  • respond to your enquiry or booking request;
  • manage your attendance at an event you've booked onto;
  • send you practical information about an event you've booked (e.g. venue details, timing changes, cancellations);
  • keep records for our own accounting and legal obligations.

We don't send marketing emails unless you've clearly opted in to hear from us, and we don't sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

4. Our legal basis for using your data

We rely on the following legal bases under UK GDPR:

  • Contract — to fulfil a ticket booking or respond to a booking-related enquiry;
  • Legitimate interests — to respond to general enquiries and run our business effectively, in a way that doesn't override your own rights and interests;
  • Consent — where you've opted in to marketing communications, which you can withdraw at any time.

5. Who we share your data with

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to run this website and our events:

  • FormSubmit — processes and forwards messages submitted through our website enquiry form to our inbox;
  • Eventbrite — handles ticket sales, payment and attendee registration for our events, as a separate data controller under its own privacy policy;
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website.

We don't share your data with any other third party except where we're required to by law.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep enquiry and booking-related correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with your enquiry or event, and afterwards only as long as needed for our accounting, tax and legal obligations, after which it's deleted.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • ask us what personal data we hold about you, and get a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data;
  • ask us to delete your data, in certain circumstances;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, certain uses of your data;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we're relying on consent (e.g. marketing);
  • complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), if you're unhappy with how we've handled your data.

To exercise any of these rights, just email us at hello@houseofkaos.co.uk.

8. Keeping your data safe

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal data we hold, and only the House of Kaos team has access to enquiry correspondence. No online service is ever 100% secure, but we don't store payment details ourselves, and enquiry data is limited to what you choose to tell us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version published on this page is the one that applies, so please check back occasionally.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, please get in touch at hello@houseofkaos.co.uk.
