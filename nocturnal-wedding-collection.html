The Nocturnal Wedding Collection

Gothic Romance, Staged With Intent

Your venue, your caterer, your day. We make it unforgettable: dark florals, candlelit staging, thrones and heart arches in the deepest palettes, and costumed cast to bring the room to life.

The Nocturnal Wedding Collection is House of Kaos working with Hearts in Bloom, our wedding styling studio. We dress the ceremony, transform the room and, if you want them, add performers. You choose the venue and the food. We handle the atmosphere.
