The Keeper of the House

Vespera

She lights the candles, she minds the doors, and she remembers every soul who crosses the threshold.

They ask how long I have kept this House. I tell them I stopped counting candles long ago.

My name means the evening star. I wake at dusk, I light the candles, and I mind the doors until dawn. The House stands at the edge of the city for those who go looking for it, and it has many doors. Each one opens onto a different world.

I have opened them one by one, and closed them again when the night was done. And I remember everyone who came through.

— Vespera

The Door Not Yet Opened

Now I stand before a door that drips with seawater, and I can hear singing on the other side. On 25 September 2027 it opens onto the Siren’s Abyss Ball.

And when the grand doors are closed, I keep a smaller fire burning: the Wakes for the Living, where the House’s guests gather by candlelight, dressed in black, to keep each other company.

The candles are lit and the kettle is warm. Come in. The House remembers everyone who crosses its threshold.
