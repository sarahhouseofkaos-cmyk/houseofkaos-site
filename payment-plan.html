Payment Plan

A simple way to pay for your ticket in instalments

This isn't an automated checkout — every payment plan is set up by hand between you and House of Kaos. Send a request below and we'll come back to you with a personalised schedule within a couple of working days.

How It Works

Step One

Request

Fill in the form below with the event, how many tickets, and which plan length suits you. It only takes a minute.

Step Two

Review

We'll reply within a couple of working days with a personalised quote — how much, how often, and when your final instalment is due.

Step Three

Pay & Confirm

Once you're happy with the schedule, we'll agree how instalments are paid and confirm each one as it's received. Your place is held from your first payment and fully confirmed once the plan is paid off.

Plan Lengths

Pick whichever suits your budget — just mention it in your request and we'll work the schedule out around it.

  • 4 weeks — for guests booking closer to the date;
  • 8 weeks — our most popular option;
  • 12 weeks — for the longest run-up to a ball or dinner.

Need something different? Tell us in your message and we'll see what we can do.

The Fine Print

Payment plans are offered directly by House of Kaos rather than through Eventbrite, so they're covered by our own Terms & Conditions. A few things to know before you request one:

  • Your place is held as soon as your first instalment is received, but only confirmed and ticketed once the plan is paid in full;
  • Instalments are arranged directly between you and House of Kaos — we'll agree the schedule and payment method with you when we send your quote;
  • Plans need to be paid off before the event date, so please get in touch as early as you can to leave enough time;
  • As with all direct bookings, instalments are non-refundable, but your place can be transferred to someone else with advance notice.
