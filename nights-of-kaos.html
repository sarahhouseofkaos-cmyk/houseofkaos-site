The Evening

Not every House of Kaos night is a grand ball or a staged dinner. Nights of Kaos are the gatherings in between — smaller, informal evenings where the House's growing community meets simply to be together, without the scale or structure of the bigger occasions.

These are the nights where ideas for the next ball first get talked about, where new faces meet regulars, and where the atmosphere is closer to a gathering of friends than a staged production — though the House's gothic sensibility is never far away.

The evenings between the grand occasions — where the House's community gathers, and the next story begins to take shape.

Who They're For

Nights of Kaos are open to anyone drawn to House of Kaos's world, whether you've been to every grand ball or you're simply curious and haven't yet made it to one. There's no elaborate costume expected — just an appetite for good company and a slightly darker sense of occasion than most nights out.

Why They Matter

The grand balls and themed dinners are what House of Kaos is known for, but the community that makes them possible is built in between — at gatherings like these. Nights of Kaos are where that community lives day to day.
