How It Works

Three Steps

1. The Consultation

Tell us the occasion, the guest list and the budget, over coffee or over something stronger.

2. The World

We design everything: theme, menu, décor, performers, music and the small theatrical moments in between. Nothing is decided without you, and nothing is left to chance.

3. The Night

We stage it all: venue, styling, dining, performance. You and your guests walk in, and the world is already alive.
